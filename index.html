THE PINK
BANANA
MANIFESTO

"If a banana is not pink, is it even a banana? And if it is, who told you that?"

🍌 🌸 🍌 🌸 🍌
ACT I — PEEL ONE

In Which We Discover That Yellow Was a Lie

A single spotlight illuminates a banana on a velvet cushion. It is unmistakably pink. A SCIENTIST enters from stage left, visibly distressed. A BANANA PHILOSOPHER sits cross-legged upstage, peeling nothing.
SCIENTIST

But the carotenoids... the chlorophyll degradation... the xanthophylls...

BANANA PHILOSOPHER

You measured the banana. But did you listen to the banana?

SCIENTIST

Bananas don't talk.

BANANA PHILOSOPHER

And yet here we are. In a play. About one.

(The banana does not comment. It has seen worse.)

ACT II — PEEL TWO

The Testimony of the Strawberry

A STRAWBERRY takes the witness stand. It is wearing a tiny monocle. The JURY is composed entirely of grapes. They are not impartial.
PROSECUTOR (A LEMON)

Ms. Strawberry. You claim to have always known the banana was pink?

STRAWBERRY

Of course. We're in the same fruit bowl every Thursday. The banana is pink. The apple knows it. The kiwi knows it. Everyone knows it.

PROSECUTOR

Then why does every painting, every emoji, every children's book show it as yellow?

STRAWBERRY

(adjusting monocle) Propaganda.

(Three grapes faint. The foreman, a particularly round Muscat, calls for a recess.)

~ INTERMISSION ~

Please visit the lobby for complimentary pink lemonade.
The management reminds you that flash photography
may distress the bananas.

The bananas have feelings.
Pink feelings.

ACT III — PEEL THREE

A Brief History of Chromatic Suppression

A HISTORIAN enters carrying a scroll that is clearly just a rolled-up tablecloth. Behind them, a projection shows increasingly unhinged conspiracy diagrams connecting Big Yellow to the banana industry.
HISTORIAN

In 1923, the United Fruit Company held an emergency board meeting. Subject: the banana's true colour had been photographed for the first time. In pink.

HISTORIAN

The photograph was destroyed. The photographer was given a lifetime supply of yellow paint and a one-way ticket to a country without bananas.

HISTORIAN

That country? Iceland.

ACT IV — PEEL FOUR

The Banana Speaks

Complete darkness. A single pink spotlight slowly rises. The BANANA is centre stage. It has been silent for three acts. The audience holds its breath. A grape coughs.
THE BANANA

...

THE BANANA

.....

THE BANANA

I am what I am.

THE BANANA

I did not ask to be categorised. I did not ask to be debated. I did not ask to be placed on cereal boxes next to a cartoon toucan who, frankly, has his own colour issues.

THE BANANA

I am pink. I have always been pink. Your denial is not my problem. It is yours.

(Standing ovation from the strawberry. The lemon quietly leaves.)
ACT V — THE FINAL PEEL

In Which Nothing Is Resolved

All characters return to stage. The BANANA is now wearing a small crown made of petals. The SCIENTIST has switched to philosophy. The HISTORIAN has eaten the scroll. The grapes have formed a union.
BANANA PHILOSOPHER

So. What have we learned?

SCIENTIST (NOW PHILOSOPHER)

That colour is a construct, truth is negotiable, and fruit is political.

STRAWBERRY

And that I look fantastic in a monocle.

THE BANANA

(removing crown, placing it gently on the floor)
Go home. Eat a banana. Notice the colour. Or don't.

It was never about the colour.

EVERYONE

Then what was it about?

THE BANANA

Potassium.

Blackout. A single pink petal falls from the ceiling. The audience is unsure whether to clap or call someone. They do both.

Sign the Petition

We, the undersigned, demand the immediate reclassification of the banana from "yellow" to "pink (and always has been)."

T. Banana, Esq.

FIN.

"The banana is pink. The theatre is empty. The meaning was inside you the whole time. Specifically in your pancreas. That's where the potassium goes."
